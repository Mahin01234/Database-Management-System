12. find all department that do not have any sudent 
Ans: 
select department_name 
from departments
left join students 
on departments.department_id = students.department_id 
where
students.student_id is null ; 



13. show all departments along with their course , including departments that have no courses. 
Ans:
select department_name , course_name 
from 
departments
left join courses 
on departments.department_id = course.department_id ;
